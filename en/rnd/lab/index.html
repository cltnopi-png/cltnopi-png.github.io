Introduction

CoreLinkTech R&D center is actively striving for high-strength and advanced HPF. We perform many R&D projects and various research activities to improve cycle time and safety of HPF. We have excellent manpower, advanced CAD programs and test equipments to self-manufacture from simulation to design and manufacturing. We make an effort to play a pivotal role in 21C press tooling engineering through know-how of accumulated tooling manufacturing engineering and continuous engineering development.

National R&D project: TWB hot stamping dies for one-piece door rings

A 4-year national R&D project with KITECH and Autogen. Project years 1 to 4, in order.

  1. Year 1: Modeling and forming analysisConcept design based on side-sill geometry; process conditions defined; patent data secured.
  2. Year 2: Lab-scale die and TWB samplesForming and property analysis by thickness; production-scale design including cooling channels.
  3. Year 3: Production-scale TWB dieProcess stabilized by forming analysis; door ring die completed; 1 patent registered.
  4. Year 4: Quality improvementTWB door ring dies validated; casting dies and samples produced by 3D printing.
