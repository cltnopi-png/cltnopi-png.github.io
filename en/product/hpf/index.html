Development process

Steps 01–06, from forming simulation to CMM and line support, are done in-house.

  1. Forming simulationFormability, thinning and forming force before any steel is cut
  2. Die & cooling designCooling channels designed and checked by flow analysis
  3. Machining3 machining centers and 1 high-speed mill; beds up to 4,000 mm
  4. Spotting & assemblyDie surface fitting (spotting) and assembly before try-out
  5. In-house try-outFurnaces, robot transfer and presses up to 1,200 t
  6. CMM & line support3D CMM inspection; on-site support at overseas PHS production lines
